# 🥯 BAGLE — It's All About The Bag

A ridiculous fantasy trading game. Grandma left you a burlap sack and ₲120. Make it legendary.

**Play:** open `index.html` in a browser (or run `python -m http.server` and go to http://localhost:8000).
No build step, no dependencies. Progress autosaves to your browser.

## Core loop
- 🛒 **Buy** goods in the market, 🎒 **sell** by clicking items in your bag.
- 🗺️ **Travel** between 7 towns. Each one pays very different prices (dwarves are always hungry).
- 🔥 **Sale streaks** raise prices, and 🎰 **jackpots** pay ×2–×5.
- 🕵️ **Fakes** are everywhere. Sell them in strict towns and 👮 the guards might bust you. Rebel Hollow buys them with no questions asked.
- 🏴‍☠️ **Pirates**, beggars, gnome salesmen, tax collectors, rebel couriers and a Chicken of Destiny show up on the road.
- 😇 **Karma**: good deeds bring bag-god blessings. 😈 Villains get along with pirates.
- 🛍️ **Bag Shop**: 8 bags, from Grandma's Sack to THE BAGLE, plus 4 tiers of mystery bags.
- ⚒️ **Upgrades**, 📜 **contracts**, 🏆 **trophies**, and 🌟 **ascension** ("Pass the Bag") for permanent Golden Threads.

Keyboard: `S` sells everything that turns a profit. `Enter` confirms dialogs.
